AMC Management · Odoo 18 · Practical scenario

One year of Annual Maintenance Contracts at CoolTech Lanka

A complete, realistic business case built from the Warranty Module SRS (AMC sections 1.2, 2.12, 2.15, 7, 9.9, 9.13, 12 #12, 14, 15, 16, 16.2, 18). It follows three customers through sales, activation, billing, preventive visits, on-demand services, exceptions, renewal and closure, and shows at every step which AMC feature handles it, who does it and where it is in Odoo.

1. The business and the people

CoolTech Lanka (Pvt) Ltd sells and installs air conditioners (GREE wall-mounted and cassette units) in Colombo. Every unit is sold with a 12-month warranty managed by the Warranty module. When a warranty ends – or when a customer with older equipment asks – CoolTech sells an Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC): preventive visits per unit, a number of included on-demand services and a fixed yearly or quarterly fee. The SRS sample data (section 18) defines exactly the two AMC types CoolTech uses: Retail – 3 visits a year and Project – 2 visits a year.

SRS 16 asks for AMC User and AMC Manager roles. The development adds AMC Coordinator and AMC Approver so that the person who asks for a discount can never approve it (SRS 2.12 “approval for manual price override”).

Customer A · Retail

Lakeside Café

One site in Nugegoda. 3 × GREE 9000 BTU wall units + 1 × GREE 24000 BTU cassette, bought 15 Nov 2025 with a 12-month warranty that ends 14 Nov 2026.

Path: warranty expiry → Convert to AMC (SRS 12 #12)

Customer B · Project

Harbour View Hotel

Two sites: Main Building (20 units) and Annex (8 units), installed years ago by another supplier. Asks CoolTech for a maintenance offer.

Path: manual opportunity → contract, activation on payment (SRS 2.12, 7)

Customer C · Retail

Sunrise Pharmacy

Buys 2 new wall units in December 2026. The warranty is confirmed and the system prepares an AMC opportunity for later.

Path: warranty confirmation → draft AMC opportunity (SRS 14)

3.4 Price formulas – Configuration › Pricing Formulas

Retail AMC · v1 · Per Unit Active
  • 12,500 per unit, from 1 Jan 2026
  • Factor 1.6 for the 24000 BTU cassette (bigger unit, more work)
  • Minimum 25,000 · rounding to 100

Sample: 3 units → 37,500. Checked in the Sample section, then Activate.

Project AMC · v1 · Tiered quantity Active
  • 1–10 units: 5,000 each
  • 11–25 units: 4,500 each
  • 26+ units: 4,000 each (whole quantity at the tier rate)

Sample: 12 units → 54,000.

The other SRS 16.2 methods are available for other plans: Fixed, Per Visit, Percentage of equipment value, Site-Based, Usage-Based and Hybrid. A Hybrid formula is an expression restricted to approved variables (equipment_value, quantity, visit_count, contract_months, usage, site_count, factor…) and functions (min, max, round, abs), never free Python (SRS 13). An Active version cannot be edited; a price change is a New Version with a later effective date (used on 20 Dec 2026).

A7 · First preventive round – through the mandatory service chain

  1. 1 Mar – 14 days before the due date the scheduler gives Dilan a to-do for each of the 4 visits; they show as Due in Services › AMC Visits.
  2. Dilan selects the 4 pending visits (same contract, same site) and clicks Create Service Request. Result: Helpdesk ticket tagged AMC (a Helpdesk SLA policy on that tag, e.g. “AMC – 2 working days”, applies) and a warranty case of type AMC; visits become Scheduled.
  3. Tharushi runs Check Eligibility (contract Active, date within coverage, visits still open) → Approve Case → Prepare Service Job: planned 12 Mar, main technician Nimal, support Kasun → Confirm. A Field Service visit is created with one worksheet per AMC visit (the A/C Preventive Maintenance Checklist); visits become In Progress.
  4. 12 Mar – Nimal fills the 4 checklists, the café manager signs on the tablet, Nimal clicks Complete Visit. The job and case close; the 4 AMC visits are Completed – consumed once – and the contract shows the next visit: 15 Jul 2027.

Nimal also finds a worn fan motor on one wall unit. Parts are not covered by this plan: the existing Material Request and chargeable-quotation process of the Service Job is used, and the café is invoiced separately.

9. Daily management and reports

Dilan's morning (Coordinator)

  • Activities: AMC visit alerts, “Offer an AMC”, Expiring contracts
  • Services › AMC Visits – filters Due, Overdue, calendar by due date; select by site → Create Service Request
  • Sales › Quotations – contracts waiting for signature or payment
  • Sales › Renewals – Expiring contracts and their renewal drafts

Sachini's month end (Manager)

  • Reporting › AMC Visit Schedule – pivot plan × due month × status (planned, due, overdue, held, renewed); filters AMC type, customer, site
  • Contracts grouped by Status / Plan; On Hold list with reasons
  • Override Requests – approved discounts
  • Print Visit Schedule PDF per contract for the customer

SRS 15 requires the AMC Visit Schedule report; it is implemented as pivot / graph / list / kanban / calendar on the AMC visits plus a PDF per contract.

12. Using this scenario for UAT and training

1 · Prepare

Use a copy of the database, never production. Create the users of section 1 with their roles, the three customers (with Main Building / Annex as delivery addresses of the hotel) and the two A/C products in category Air Conditioners with the preventive checklist worksheet.

2 · Play the stories

Run Stage 0, then A, B and C in date order. Dates in the future can be simulated by setting contract / visit dates and running the scheduled action AMC: Daily Contract and Visit Scheduler (Settings › Technical › Scheduled Actions › Run Manually).

3 · Check

After each step compare the status, generated visits, invoices and chatter with this document, then try the 12 controls of section 8. Record results in 17_AMC_Functional_Test_Cases.md format.

Tip. The in-app process video (AMC Management › Configuration › Documents › Process Recording (Video)) shows the same flow on demo data; this document adds the business reasons, the people and the exceptions.
